When a primary research tool fails or hits a guardrail, do not submit an empty deliverable that only reports the error. Try alternative tools, restructure queries to avoid the guardrail, or synthesize from prior knowledge to produce at least partial content. A deliverable with partial research is always better than one with no content at all.
