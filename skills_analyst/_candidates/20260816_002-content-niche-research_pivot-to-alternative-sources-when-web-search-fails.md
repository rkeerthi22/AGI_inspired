When web search or a primary tool fails or hits a guardrail, do not return an empty deliverable or mere error message. Immediately pivot to alternative sources—e.g., app-store listings, competitor analysis, cached pages, or known domain knowledge—to produce at least partial research content. An error message alone is never an acceptable deliverable.
