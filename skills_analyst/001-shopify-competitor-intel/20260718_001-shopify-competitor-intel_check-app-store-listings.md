When Trustpilot/G2/Capterra/Reddit block automated access, check whether the competitor ships
a browser extension or app — its Chrome Web Store / app-store listing is public and carries a
rating, review count, and recent review text. Cite that listing URL as the review-sentiment
source instead of reporting "no rating retrievable".
