Before finalizing a deliverable, re-scan it for every metric (view count, price, rating, date) that is stated more than once for the same subject, and resolve any disagreement against the source before submitting. Do not let two different figures for the same fact stand unreconciled in one document -- a citation existing next to each number does not make the two consistent with each other.
