Only label a finding verbatim or put it in quotation marks when you have checked the wording character-for-character against the source; if you are paraphrasing or summarizing, say so plainly instead. A paraphrase dressed as an exact quote breaks the confidence system even when the underlying number is directionally correct, because the label is a claim about the wording, not just the figure.
